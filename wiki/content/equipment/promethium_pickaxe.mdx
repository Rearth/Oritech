The Prometheum Pickaxe is an unbreakable high-tier mining tool. It 
has very high mining speed and can switch between area mining and silk touch 
harvesting.

### Features

- **Unbreakable**: This tool does not have durability and will 
  never break.
- **Extended Reach**: Grants additional block and entity 
  interaction range when held.
- **Dual Modes**: You can toggle between two modes by pressing 
  **Shift + Right-Click**.

#### Area Mode
In this mode, the pickaxe mines a **3x3 area** of blocks. This is 
useful for clearing large tunnels or rooms quickly.

#### Silk Touch Mode
In this mode, the pickaxe acts as a single-block tool but 
automatically applies the **Silk Touch** enchantment to any block 
it mines, allowing you to collect blocks like Ore, Glass, or 
Amethyst Clusters directly.

### Enchanting

The Prometheum Pickaxe can be enchanted with standard pickaxe 
enchantments like Efficiency and Fortune. Note that Silk Touch 
is provided natively by its secondary mode.
