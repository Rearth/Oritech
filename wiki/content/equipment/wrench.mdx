The pipe wrench is the main tool for managing factory 
logistics. It works with all types of pipes and cables 
in the mod.

### Pipes

The wrench helps you tune your pipe networks:
- **Toggle**: Click the end of a pipe where it connects 
  to something else to turn that connection on or off.
- **Quick Pick-up**: Shift + Right-click a pipe to pick 
  it up instantly.

### Ziplining

<center>
<ModAsset location="oritech:area/cable_ziplining" width={512} />
</center>

You can use the wrench to **zipline** on the wires between 
[power poles](@oritech:power_pole_block). Just right-click 
a wire to travel quickly to the other side. 

Ziplining is a fast way to move around without using any 
energy.
