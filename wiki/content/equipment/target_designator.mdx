The target designator is a tool used to tell your machines 
where to point. You can save a position and then give 
it to a machine.

### How to use

1. **Save a position**: Right-click a block to save its 
   coordinates.
2. **Give to machine**: Shift-right-click a machine while holding 
    the designator to set its new target.

### Machines

You need the designator for these machines:

- [Laser Arm](@oritech:enderic_laser): Tells the laser 
  where to fire.
- [Drone Port](@oritech:drone_port): Sets the destination 
  for drones.
- [Power Pole](@oritech:energy_transmission_pole): Connects power 
  poles to each other.

### Hunter Addons

If you have **Hunter Addons** on a [Laser Arm](@oritech:enderic_laser), 
using the designator on the machine will change what it 
targets, such as mobs or players.

<Callout variant="info">
    You can see the saved coordinates in the item tooltip.
</Callout>
