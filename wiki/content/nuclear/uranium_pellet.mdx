Uranium and plutonium are the high-density energy sources used to power nuclear reactors.

<center>
<ModAsset location="oritech:area/uranium_cave" width={700} />
</center>

**Uranium** is naturally occurring ore found deep underground. It can be processed into fuel pellets for standard reactor operation.

**Plutonium** is a stronger fuel source obtained by destroying uranium crystals with an [enderic laser](@oritech:enderic_laser). It can also be created with
particle collisions.

Raw ores must be processed through a [pulverizer](@oritech:pulverizer) and [centrifuge](@oritech:centrifuge)
to create enriched pellets. These pellets are consumed inside [nuclear reactors]($reactor/introduction) to generate very large amounts of energy.
