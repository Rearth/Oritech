title: Neutron Reflector
id: oritech:reactor_reflector

---

Neutron reflectors will reflect the neutrons of neighboring fuel rods, increasing their efficiency.

When placed adjacent to a [reactor rod](@oritech:reactor_rod), the reflector bounces neutron pulses back into the rod stack.
This simulates the presence of an additional fuel rod neighbor, increasing both the energy output and the heat generation
of the affected rod without consuming any extra fuel.

For detailed information on reactor mechanics, see the [nuclear reactor guide]($reactor/introduction).
