Flux gates are high-tier control parts used where Oritech needs to regulate large amounts of power.

**Crafting**
- **Assembler**: Constructed from a [Processing Unit](@oritech:processing_unit), [Fluxite](@oritech:fluxite), 
and [Platinum](@oritech:platinum_ingot).

**Usage**
- Used in late mid-game and end-game blocks such as the [Particle Accelerator](@oritech:particle_accelerator), 
[Atomic Forge](@oritech:atomic_forge), [Large Solar Panel](@oritech:big_solar_panel), 
[Bio Generator](@oritech:bio_generator), [Superconductor](@oritech:superconductor), 
and [Advanced Augmentation Station](@oritech:quantum_research_station).
- It is also common in high-tier addons and containment recipes, so it often becomes one of the first advanced 
components players mass-produce.
