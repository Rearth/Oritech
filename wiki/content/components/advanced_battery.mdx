- **Centrifuge**: Can be mass-produced by processing [Dubious Containers](@oritech:dubious_container) with [Sulfuric Acid](@oritech:fluid_sulfuric_acid).

**Usage**
- They are used in equipment such as the [Electric Mace](@oritech:electric_mace), [Portable Laser](@oritech:portable_laser), jet-assisted armor and flight equipment, and in storage or power blocks such as the [Large Storage Block](@oritech:large_storage_block) and [Large Solar Panel](@oritech:big_solar_panel_block).
- Once you automate sulfuric acid, centrifuging filled [Dubious Containers](@oritech:dubious_container) is the practical way to scale production.
