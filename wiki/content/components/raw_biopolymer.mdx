An organic precursor to industrial plastics made from biological matter.

**Crafting**
- **Centrifuge**: Extracted from [Packed Wheat](@oritech:packed_wheat), [Solid Biofuel](@oritech:solid_biofuel), 
or Biomass Blocks using Water.

**Usage**
Used as a base for plastics, specialized fluids, and biological augments.
