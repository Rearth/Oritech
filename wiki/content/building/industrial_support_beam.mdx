title: Metal Beam
type: block
---

Beams are structural blocks used to support large factories and industrial buildings.
They work well for framing machines and rooms.

The metal beam connects to blocks or beams above and below.

Sneak-right-click a beam with **[machine paint](@oritech:orange_paint)** to recolor it.
Gray metal parts, including the middle section, stay gray. The chosen color is preserved when neighboring supports change.
