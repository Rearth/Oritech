The Industrial Hangar Door is a large animated door for factory gates, maintenance shafts, and cargo access.
It is a 1x3x1 door that can be placed on floors, ceilings, and walls.

Unlike the **[industrial door](@oritech:tech_door)**, the hangar door can only be controlled by redstone.
Neighboring hangar doors can be daisy-chained, letting one redstone signal open an entire connected row.

When placed on walls, it is facing vertical when placed from above/below. It is facing horizontal when placed from the sides.

It matches **[machine plating](@oritech:machine_plating_block)** and can also be recolored with industrial paints.
