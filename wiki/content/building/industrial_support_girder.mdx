Girders are support beams made for horizontal spans and structural detailing.
Their truss-like shape works well for bridges, crane supports, and roof structures.

Like beams, metal girders connect to adjacent blocks to form continuous structures.

Sneak-right-click a girder with **[machine paint](@oritech:orange_paint)** to recolor it.
Gray metal parts stay gray. The chosen color is preserved when neighboring girders change.
