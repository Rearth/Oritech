Oritech includes several plating blocks for factory construction.
They work well for floors, walls, and structural casings.

All plating blocks come with **slab**, **stair** and **pressure plate** variants.

### Plating Variants

*   **[Machine Plating](@oritech:copper_reinforced_plating)**: The standard plating used for machine casings.
It has a clean, smooth texture that fits well with Oritech machines.
*   **[Iron Plating](@oritech:iron_plating)**: A darker, rugged industrial-grade plating.
Good for heavy industry areas and structural supports.
*   **[Nickel Plating](@oritech:nickel_plating)**: A bright, corrosion-resistant plating with a slightly yellowish tint.
Useful for clean rooms or specialized equipment areas.
*   **[Carbon Plating](@oritech:carbon_plating)**: A modern, high-strength dark plating.
Useful for contrast and darker advanced facility designs.
