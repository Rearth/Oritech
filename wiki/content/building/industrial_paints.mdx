Most Oritech machines, especially the animated ones, can be repainted with different color schemes.
Machine paints come in 9 variants, each with a slightly different style.

Paints also work on these decorative blocks:

* **[Industrial Door](@oritech:industrial_door)**
* **[Industrial Hangar Door](@oritech:hangar_door)**
* **[Metal Beam](@oritech:industrial_support_beam)**
* **[Metal Girder](@oritech:industrial_support_girder)**

To apply a new color, sneak-right-click a supported block while holding the paint.
Each color change consumes one paint item and plays the normal hand animation, a splash sound, and particles.
Using the same color again does nothing and consumes no paint.

You can paint either section of an industrial door or any segment of a hangar door.
Beams and girders are painted one block at a time. Their gray metal parts stay gray, including the beam's middle section.
Paint is preserved when neighboring supports are added or removed. Orange paint restores the original appearance.

Other decorative blocks, including plating, industrial glass, lights, buttons, and levers, cannot currently be painted.
