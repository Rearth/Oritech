The Quantum Research Station is a high-tier expansion for the 
[Augmentation Center](@oritech:augment_application_block). 
It is required to research endgame augmentations that deal with 
space-time and high-energy physics.

It also requires **3 machine cores** and occupies one of the center's research station slots.

This tier unlocks stronger technical augments and upgraded versions of earlier ones, such as stronger health boosts, better armor, far reach, night vision, hyper mining, and high-speed movement augments.
