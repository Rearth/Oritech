The Crop Filter Addon is used for automated farming.
When attached to a mining machine, it filters targets to
avoid destroying immature crops or animals.

This addon is applicable to:
- **[Block Destroyer](@oritech:destroyer_block)**
- **[Enderic Laser](@oritech:laser_arm_block)** (works for both block destruction and animal filtering with the hunter addon)

---

**Crop Filtering**

The filter makes sure the machine only breaks crops that are fully
grown and ready for harvest.
It also prevents the machine from accidentally killing passive mobs
or farm animals within its working area.
