The Acceptor Addon is an energy module that provides an
external connection point for energy systems.

Applicable to all blocks with energy.

---

**External Charging**

Unlike standard machines which usually only accept energy at their base,
the Acceptor Addon allows energy to be inserted directly into the
addon block itself.
It adds 500 000 RF of capacity and a high throughput of 5 000 RF/t.
Use this addon when the machine's main power inputs are hard to 
reach or already occupied.
