The Tree Cutter is a machine for large-scale 
timber harvesting. 
When powered, it will automatically detect and cut down entire trees 
in front of it.

---

**Automated Forestry**

The machine processes trees block by block, including all connected 
logs and leaves. 
Drops from the leaves, such as saplings or fruit, are 
automatically collected into the machine's internal inventory. 
It can handle massive trees up to 8,000 blocks in size.
