Refinery Chamber Modules are expansion components for the 
[Industrial Refinery](@oritech:refinery). 
Up to two modules can be stacked on top of a single refinery block 
to enable additional fluid output channels.

---

**Multi-Output Management**

Each module acts as a dedicated tank for the secondary and tertiary 
fluid products of a refining process. 
Fluids must be extracted directly from the module blocks rather than 
the main refinery base. 
These modules also require their own 
[machine cores]($multiblocks) to be part of the multiblock
assembly.
