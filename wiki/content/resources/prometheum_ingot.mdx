> Stolen fire from the gods

Prometheum is Oritech's final endgame material.
It can only be produced in an [atomic forge](@oritech:atomic_forge_block) and marks the last major step in progression.

It is used to craft [prometheum tools](@oritech:promethium_pickaxe)
and the final Tier 7 machine core.
