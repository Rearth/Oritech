Resource nodes are infinite sources of raw materials that can be mined using the
[Bedrock Extractor](@oritech:deep_drill_block). These nodes are found embedded in bedrock
and are indestructible.

To find these nodes, look for [Ore Boulders](@oritech:world_ores) on the surface.
They mark a resource node directly below in the bedrock.

<center>
<ModAsset location="oritech:area/resource_node" width={700} />
</center>

Resource nodes exist for several materials, including iron, copper, gold,
nickel, platinum, uranium, and even rare gems like diamonds.
Mining these nodes requires the drill to reach the bedrock layer.
