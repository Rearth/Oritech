Platinum is a valuable resource used for high-end electronics,
advanced alloys like [duratium](@oritech:duratium_ingot), and top-tier machinery.

In the overworld, platinum ore generates deep underground between **Y -60 and Y -20**.
It is also found much more abundantly throughout **The End**.

Platinum can also be obtained by processing [nickel](@oritech:nickel_ingot)
in a [fragment forge](@oritech:fragment_forge_block).
