Duratium is a high-tier alloy used to create [particle accelerators]($particle_accelerator)
and [reactors]($reactor/introduction). It is a very dense material.

Duratium can be made in two ways:
- In the [foundry](@oritech:foundry_block) by alloying [platinum](@oritech:platinum_ingot) and Netherite.
- In the [atomic forge](@oritech:atomic_forge_block) using [platinum](@oritech:platinum_ingot)
and [reinforced carbon sheets](@oritech:reinforced_carbon_sheet).
