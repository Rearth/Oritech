A durable sheet made from treated carbon fibres.

**Crafting**
- **Refinery**: Created by treating [Carbon Fibre Strands](@oritech:carbon_fibre_strands) with 
[Naphtha](@oritech:fluid_naphtha).

**Usage**
Used for plating and propulsion components.
