An ultra-durable sheet made from chemically treated carbon fibres.

**Crafting**
- **Refinery**: Created by treating [Carbon Fibre Strands](@oritech:carbon_fibre_strands) with [Naphtha](@oritech:fluid_naphtha).

**Usage**
A high-strength structural material for plating and propulsion components.
