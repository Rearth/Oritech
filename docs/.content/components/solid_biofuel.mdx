A compressed, long-lasting fuel briquette made from biomass.

**Crafting**
- **Assembler**: Compacted from 3 [Biomass](@oritech:biomass) and Planks.

**Usage**
A highly efficient, sustainable fuel source for bio-reactors and chemical processing.
