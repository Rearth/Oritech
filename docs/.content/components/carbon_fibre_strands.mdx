A strong, light material made from carbon-rich dusts.

**Crafting**
- **Centrifuge**: Produced by processing [Coal Dusts](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Coal) or similar carbon-based dusts.

**Usage**
Used to make reinforced sheets, levers, and mid-tier machine cores.
