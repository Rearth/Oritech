Flux gates are high-tier control components used anywhere Oritech needs to regulate dense power throughput.

**Crafting**
- **Assembler**: Constructed from a [Processing Unit](@oritech:processing_unit), [Fluxite](@oritech:fluxite), 
and [Platinum](@oritech:platinum_ingot).

**Usage**
- Used in late mid-game and end-game blocks such as the [Particle Accelerator](@oritech:accelerator_controller), 
[Atomic Forge](@oritech:atomic_forge_block), [Large Solar Panel](@oritech:big_solar_panel_block), 
[Bio Generator](@oritech:bio_generator_block), [Superconductor](@oritech:superconductor), 
and [Advanced Augmentation Station](@oritech:advanced_augment_station).
- It is also a common ingredient in high-tier addons and containment recipes, so it often becomes the first advanced 
component players mass-produce.
