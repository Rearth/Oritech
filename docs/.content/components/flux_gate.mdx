A specialized component for regulating and directing high-energy flux.

**Crafting**
- **Assembler/Crafting Table**: Constructed from a [Processing Unit](@oritech:processing_unit), [Fluxite](@oritech:fluxite), and [Platinum Ingots](@oritech:platinum_ingot).

**Usage**
Regulates high-energy flows in advanced machinery, particle accelerators, and superconducting systems.
