A powerful propulsion unit that utilizes ion acceleration for thrust.

**Crafting**
- **Assembler/Crafting Table**: Assembled from [Reinforced Carbon Sheets](@oritech:reinforced_carbon_sheet), [Advanced Batteries](@oritech:advanced_battery), and [Flux Gates](@oritech:flux_gate).

**Usage**
Provides propulsion and acceleration for flight systems and particle accelerators.
