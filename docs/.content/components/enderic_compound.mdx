A stable compound infused with the teleportation properties of the End.

**Crafting**
- **Pulverizer/Grinder**: Created by crushing [Ender Pearls](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Ender_Pearl) or 
[End Stone](https://minecraft.wiki/w/End_Stone).
- **Centrifuge**: Extracted from [Sculk](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sculk) blocks.

**Usage**
Used for teleportation technology, advanced plating, and Tier 4 machine cores.
