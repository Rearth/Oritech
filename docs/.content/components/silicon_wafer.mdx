A delicate slice of purified silicon used as a base for microprocessors.

**Crafting**
- **Atomic Forge**: Fabricated from [Carbon Fibre Strands](@oritech:carbon_fibre_strands) and [Silicon](@oritech:silicon).
- **Centrifuge**: Processed from [Carbon Fibre Strands](@oritech:carbon_fibre_strands) and 
[Silicon Wash](@oritech:fluid_silicon_wash).

**Usage**
The foundation for advanced computing and artificial intelligence circuits.
