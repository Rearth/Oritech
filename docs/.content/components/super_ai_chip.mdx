The Super AI Chip is one of Oritech's final computing components.

**Crafting**
- **[Atomic Forge](@oritech:atomic_forge_block)**: Fused from [Duratium Ingots](@oritech:duratium_ingot) 
and [Advanced Computing Engines](@oritech:advanced_computing_engine).

**Usage**
- Used in high-tier recipes such as the [Dubious Container](@oritech:dubios_container), 
[Auxiliary Processing Chamber Addon](@oritech:machine_processing_addon), advanced drone logistics, 
and other end-game machine parts.
