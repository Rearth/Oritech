Enderic lenses focus enderic and optical effects into compact machine parts, making them a recurring ingredient in sensors, 
lasers, and perception augments.

**Crafting**
- **Assembler**: Crafted using [Adamant](@oritech:adamant_ingot), [Carbon Fibre Strands](@oritech:carbon_fibre_strands), 
and [Enderic Compound](@oritech:enderic_compound).

**Usage**
- Used in several cybernetic augments such as Night Vision, Ore Vision, Cloak, and Far Reach.
- Also appears in machine recipes that rely on targeting or sensing, including the [Laser Arm](@oritech:laser_arm_block), 
[Fuel Generator](@oritech:fuel_generator_block), and yield-focused upgrades.
