---
title: Accelerator Speed Sensor
id: oritech:accelerator_sensor
type: block
---

A particle's speed can be measured with a particle sensor.

A comparator can then provide a redstone signal based on the particle's speed. The signal strength increases
with higher particle speeds, ranging from level 1 at 0 m/s to level 15 at 15000 m/s.

Sensors can be used as straight guides in the accelerator path.

For detailed information on particle acceleration mechanics, see the [particle accelerator documentation]($PARTICLE_ACCELERATOR).
