The exo suit is an early game tech armor set designed for exploration 
and industrial operations. Each piece gives you a different useful 
ability.

### Armor pieces

- **Exo Helmet**: Gives you **Night Vision** while you wear it.
- **Exo Chestplate**: Charges energy-powered tools in your inventory 
  automatically (like the [chainsaw](@oritech:chainsaw) or 
  [hand drill](@oritech:hand_drill)).
- **Exo Leggings**: Makes you **run faster**.
- **Exo Boots**: No more **Fall Damage** and increases how far 
  you can fall safely.

### Energy & Upgrades

The armor is unbreakable but needs energy to work. You can 
charge the pieces in a [charger](@oritech:charger_block) 
or any energy storage block.

Each piece can be enchanted with standard armor enchantments like 
**Protection** and **Unbreaking**. 

The chestplate is great for long mining trips since it 
keeps your tools powered from its own battery. You can 
also use the suit with a [jetpack](@oritech:jetpack) 
to fly around.
