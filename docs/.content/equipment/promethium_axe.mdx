The Prometheum Battleaxe is an unbreakable multi-purpose harvesting 
tool. It works as a strong axe for stripping wood and 
attacking enemies, and it can also fell 
entire trees in one action.

### Features

- **Unbreakable**: Like other Prometheum tools, this axe does 
  not have durability and will never break.
- **Tree Felling**: By **Sneaking (Shift)** while harvesting a 
  log, the Battleaxe will automatically break the entire tree 
  above the targeted block. The logs and leaves will be 
  collected and dropped at the base.
- **Extended Reach**: Grants additional block and entity 
  interaction range when held.
- **Combat**: It deals significant damage, making it a viable 
  weapon in addition to a tool.
- **Standard Axe Actions**: It can be used to strip logs, 
  scrape copper, and perform all other standard axe functions.

### Enchanting

The Prometheum Battleaxe can be enchanted with standard axe 
enchantments like Efficiency, Sharpness, and Fortune.
