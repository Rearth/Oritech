The portable laser is a powerful tool you can use for 
mining and fighting from a distance. It has two modes 
you can switch between.

### Modes

Press **Shift + Right Click** to switch modes.

- **Mining Mode**: Fires a beam that breaks blocks from far 
  away. You can also use it to charge [machine cores](@oritech:machine_frame_block) 
  or speed up some machines. Like a mini enderic laser in your hand.
- **Combat Mode**: Fires shots that cause small explosions. 
  It deals a lot of damage and can turn creepers into 
  charged creepers.

### Features

- **Range**: The laser can reach blocks up to 128 blocks away.
- **Upgrades**: You can use the Efficiency enchantment 
  to make the mining mode faster. Enchantments from pickaxes and 
  also Sharpness will work on the laser.
- **Energy Saver**: Applying **Unbreaking** will reduce the 
  energy consumed by both the mining beam and combat shots.

### Energy

The laser uses a lot of energy. Charge it in a 
[charger](@oritech:charger_block) or with an 
[exo chestplate](@oritech:exo_suit).

<Callout variant="danger">
    Be careful with combat mode around your base, 
    as the explosions can break your blocks!
</Callout>
