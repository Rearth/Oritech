Plastic is a versatile polymer used for insulators, casings, and various mid-to-late game components.

There are two primary ways to produce plastic in Oritech:
1. **Bio Path**: Process [packed wheat](@oritech:biomass) in a [centrifuge](@oritech:centrifuge_block)
with water to obtain raw biopolymer, which can then be centrifuged again with water to produce plastic sheets.
2. **Oil Path**: Refine [crude oil](@oritech:fuel_generator_block) in a [refinery](@oritech:refinery_block)
to obtain naphtha. Process naphtha in a centrifuge with sand to get polymer resin,
which can be smelted into plastic sheets.

Plastic is essential for high-tier machine parts and advanced automation components.
