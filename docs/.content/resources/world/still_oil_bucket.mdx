Crude oil is a valuable liquid resource used in various industrial processes. 
It can be refined in a [Refinery](@oritech:refinery_block) to produce fuel and other useful materials.

## Finding Oil

Large oil reservoirs can be found on the surface, where they can be seen gushing from the ground.

<center>
<ModAsset location="oritech:area/oil_well" width={700} />
</center>

Oil deposits are much more common in **desert biomes**, making these arid regions prime 
targets for industrial exploitation—we mean, development.

## Extraction

Oil can be collected using buckets or pumped directly using a [Pump](@oritech:pump_block).
Once extracted, it can be stored in tanks and transported to a refinery for processing.
