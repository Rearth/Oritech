A purified semi-metal essential for electronics and advanced computing.

**Obtaining**
- **Refinery**: Produced by washing [Sand](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Sand) with [Silicon Wash](@oritech:fluid_silicon_wash).
- **Smelting**: Processed from [Raw Silicon](@oritech:raw_silicon).
- **Compacting**: Crafted from [Raw Silicon Blocks](@oritech:raw_silicon_block).

**Usage**
The base material for [Silicon Wafers](@oritech:silicon_wafer) and [Fluid Pipes](@oritech:fluid_pipe). Also used in [Machine Redstone Addons](@oritech:machine_redstone_addon) and [Fertilizers](@oritech:fertilizer_block).
