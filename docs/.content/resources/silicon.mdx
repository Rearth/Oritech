A purified semi-metal essential for electronics and advanced computing. Based on a combination of sand and quartz.

It is the base material for [Silicon Wafers](@oritech:silicon_wafer) and [Fluid Pipes](@oritech:fluid_pipe). Also used in [Machine Redstone Addons](@oritech:machine_redstone_addon) and [Fertilizers](@oritech:fertilizer_block).
