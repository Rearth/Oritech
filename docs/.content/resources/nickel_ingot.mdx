Nickel is one of the first resources you'll need to gather in Oritech.
Nickel ores generate in the overworld between **Y -65 and Y 40**.
Alternatively, it can be found in [ore boulders](@oritech:world_ores) on the surface.

<center>
<ModAsset location="oritech:area/ore_boulder" width={512} />
</center>

This versatile metal is used in several alloys, such as [adamant](@oritech:adamant)
and [energite](@oritech:energite), and is essential for crafting most machines and machine parts.

Processing raw nickel (or nickel ore) in a [fragment forge](@oritech:fragment_forge_block)
is an excellent way to obtain some [platinum](@oritech:platinum) as a byproduct.
