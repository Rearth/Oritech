> Stolen fire from the gods

Prometheum is the ultimate endgame material of Oritech, representing the pinnacle of technological advancement, condensed into one perfect ingot.
It can only be produced in an [atomic forge](@oritech:atomic_forge_block) and serves as the final stepping stone in your technological journey.

This powerful resource is essential for crafting [prometheum tools](@oritech:promethium_pickaxe)
and the ultimate (tier 7) machine core.
