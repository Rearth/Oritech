> Stolen fire from the gods

Prometheum is an advanced material that can only be produced in an [atomic forge](@oritech:atomic_forge_block).

This powerful resource is essential for crafting [prometheum tools](@oritech:hand_drill)
and the ultimate (tier 7) machine core.
