Biomass is an organic resource used as a sustainable fuel source for the [bio generator](@oritech:bio_generator_block).
It is also a key ingredient in the production of [biosteel](@oritech:biosteel_ingot).

You can obtain biomass by processing various organic materials, such as plants, crops, and saplings, in a [pulverizer](@oritech:pulverizer_block).

Additionally, biomass can be used as a food source in emergencies,
though it is not particularly appetizing and has similar effects to a poisonous potato.
It is also highly compostable.
