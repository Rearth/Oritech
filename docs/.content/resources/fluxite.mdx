Fluxite is a specialized material used for managing energy flux. It is a critical component
necessary for constructing advanced machines and [energite](@oritech:energite).

Fluxite can be obtained in two primary ways:
- **Amethyst Processing**: Mining an **Amethyst Cluster** with an [Enderic Laser](@oritech:laser_arm_block).
The laser will not destroy the budding amethyst, instead it will boost its growth speed.
- **Platinum Processing**: It is obtained as a byproduct when processing
[platinum clumps](@oritech:platinum) in a [centrifuge](@oritech:centrifuge_block).
