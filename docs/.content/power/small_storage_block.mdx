title: Portable Energy Storage
---

Portable energy storage that can be placed on the ground and used as a temporary power source. 
When broken, the dropped item will retain its charge.

The fill amount can be measured using a comparator. A redstone signal to the block will stop it from outputting energy.

Default energy capacity is 1M RF. Accepts energy from all sides through a 🟢 *green port* and can only output 
through the single 🔴 *red port*.
