Oritech provides various plating blocks designed for factory construction and industrial aesthetics.
These blocks are excellent for creating clean, high-tech floors, walls, and structural casings.

All plating blocks come with **slab**, **stair** and **pressure plate** variants.

### Plating Variants

*   **[Machine Plating](@oritech:machine_plating_block)**: The standard plating used for machine casings.
It has a clean, smooth texture that fits perfectly with all Oritech machines.
*   **[Iron Plating](@oritech:iron_plating_block)**: A darker, rugged industrial-grade plating.
Great for heavy industry areas and structural supports.
*   **[Nickel Plating](@oritech:nickel_plating_block)**: A bright, corrosion-resistant plating with a slightly yellowish tint.
Useful for clean rooms or specialized equipment areas.
*   **[Carbon Plating](@oritech:carbon_plating_block)**: A modern, high-strength dark plating.
Perfect for contrast and sleek, advanced facility designs.
