The ceiling light is a modern, high-efficiency lighting fixture for factories.
It provides a bright, clean light that covers a wide area, ensuring your workspace is always well-lit.

There are two variants:
*   **Flush Mount**: Placed directly against a block face.
*   **Hanging**: Suspended from a chain, perfect for high ceilings.

The light cannot be toggled.
