The Synergy Matrix Addon represents the pinnacle of machine optimization.
With the default additive addon setting, it adds 75% speed, meaning the
machine runs at 175% of its normal speed while only increasing energy
consumption by a modest 10%. A recipe that would normally take 100 ticks
will finish in about 57 ticks.

Applicable to all machines that consume energy each tick they work.

Due to its high efficiency and large speed bonus, it is significantly more
effective than stacking multiple standard speed and efficiency addons.
