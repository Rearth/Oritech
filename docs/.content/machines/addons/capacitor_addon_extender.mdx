The Power Bank Addon Extender combines the slot expansion of an
extender with a large internal energy reserve.

Applicable to all blocks with energy.

---

**Energy Buffer**

In addition to providing extra addon slots, this block adds 2 500 000 RF
to the machine's total capacity and increases energy throughput by
1 000 RF/t.
It is a good choice for powering energy-heavy machines that also
require many addons.
