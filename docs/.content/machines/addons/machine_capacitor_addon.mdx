---
title: Capacitor Addon
id: oritech:machine_capacitor_addon
type: block
---

The Capacitor Addon increases the internal energy buffer of a machine.
Each installed capacitor adds 2,000,000 RF of storage capacity.

---

**Power Stability**

In addition to increased capacity, it also improves the machine's
throughput, adding 2,000 RF/t of transfer rate.
This ensures that high-speed machines with many addons can receive
enough energy to operate without interruption.
