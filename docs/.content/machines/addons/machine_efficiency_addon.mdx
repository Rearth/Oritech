---
title: Efficiency Addon
id: oritech:machine_efficiency_addon
type: block
---

The Efficiency Addon focuses on reducing the energy footprint of your
machinery.
Installing this addon reduces the energy consumption of the machine by
20% per cycle.

This is particularly useful for machines that are constantly running or
for setups where energy generation is limited.
Multiple efficiency addons can be stacked to further minimize power usage.
