The fertilizer is used to enhance crop growth below the connected [machine frame](@oritech:machine_frame_block).
It requires water and energy to operate.

The effect on the blocks below is similar to applying bonemeal, although it is less potent.
Additionally, it hydrates the farmland below.

### Mineral Slurry

**Mineral Slurry** fluid can be used instead of water. This will significantly increase the strength of the growth effect.
Bone meal can also be inserted into the fertilizer block to increase its potency.
