Burns liquid fuels like oil or diesel to produce large amounts of energy. Default generation rate is 256 RF/t.

<Callout variant="warning">
    Energy is only output at the 2 yellow connection spots in the machine front.

</Callout>

Accepts liquid fuels through pipes or buckets.
This is a multiblock that needs [machine cores]($multiblocks). You can also insert or extract fuel by clicking
the machine with a fluid containing item.

Raw oil is enough to get it running, but refined fuels are where the machine becomes truly efficient. If you already have a refinery, this is usually the best first consumer for those upgraded fuels.

<Callout variant="info">
    Refined fuels provide much higher energy density than raw resources.
</Callout>
