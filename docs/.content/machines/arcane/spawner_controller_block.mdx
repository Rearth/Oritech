The Spawner Controller is the brain of an automated mob farming 
system. 
When combined with a [spawner cage](@oritech:spawner_cage_block), 
it can be configured to produce specific entity types using 
collected souls.

</center>

---

**Setting the Target**

The controller's target mob is determined by the first entity to 
walk across the block. 
Once set, it will consume souls (costing more for higher-HP mobs) 
to spawn copies of that entity within the cage below. 
The mob type can only be reset by breaking and replacing the 
controller.

<Callout variant="info">
    Certain powerful or unique entities are blacklisted and cannot 
    be replicated by the controller.
</Callout>
