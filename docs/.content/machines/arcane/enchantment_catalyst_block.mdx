The Arcane Catalyst is a storage vessel for soul energy. 
It is a critical support block for the 
[Stabilized Enchanter](@oritech:enchanter_block) and can also be 
used for manual "Hyper Enchanting".

---

**Soul Storage and Stabilization**

By default, a catalyst can store up to 50 souls collected from nearby 
entities. 
For advanced **Hyper Enchanting** (exceeding normal level limits), 
the catalyst must be stabilized using 
[enderic lasers](@oritech:laser_arm_block), which exponentially 
increases its storage capacity.

<Callout variant="danger">
    Keep the lasers powered! Losing stabilization while a catalyst is 
    full of souls can have catastrophic consequences for the 
    surrounding area.
</Callout>
