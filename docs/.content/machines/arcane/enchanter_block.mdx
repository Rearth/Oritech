The Stabilized Enchanter is the primary technological method for 
applying powerful enchantments to your equipment. 
Unlike the traditional enchanting table, it provides direct control 
over what enchantments are applied.

**Precision Enchanting**

The enchanter uses RF energy and [souls]($souls) to apply selected 
upgrades from its interface. 
It does not collect souls itself; it must be placed near 
[arcane catalysts](@oritech:enchantment_catalyst_block) that have 
stored energy from nearby entity deaths.

High-level enchantments require multiple soul-filled catalysts within 
range to function.
