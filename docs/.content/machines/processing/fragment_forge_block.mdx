The fragment forge is your first step in turning raw ores into 
usable ingots.
Fragmenting ore blocks creates **raw ores**, and fragmenting raw ores 
creates **ore dusts**. 

---

**Efficiency and Byproducts**

When fragmenting ore blocks and raw ores, the fragment forge will 
output more than it is given. Occasionally, it will even provide 
additional ores of a different type as a byproduct.

The production of these byproducts can be significantly enhanced by 
attaching [yield addons](@oritech:machine_yield_addon) to the machine.
