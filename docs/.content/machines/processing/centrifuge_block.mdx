---
title: Centrifuge
id: oritech:centrifuge_block
type: block
---

The centrifuge separates and transforms items through high-speed
rotation.
It can be used to extract ore dusts from ore clumps obtained from a 
[fragment forge](@oritech:fragment_forge_block), spin coal dust into 
carbon fiber strands, or process various liquids.

<Callout variant="info">
    When processing fluids, a [fluid addon](@oritech:machine_fluid_addon) 
    must be attached to the centrifuge before it can accept liquids.
</Callout>

---

**Fluid Separation**

Fluids can be pumped directly in and out of the **centrifuge block itself**, 
not the addon. The addon is only required to enable fluid processing compatibility.
Common uses include refining crude oil into different fuel types or 
processing wheat into plastic sheets.
