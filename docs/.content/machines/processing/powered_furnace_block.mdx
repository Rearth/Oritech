The Powered Furnace is a modern replacement for the traditional 
stone furnace. It uses RF energy to smelt items at a much higher 
speed and efficiency.

---

**Industrial Smelting**

Unlike standard furnaces, the powered furnace can be upgraded with 
[addons]($addons) to increase its throughput or reduce its power 
consumption. It is the backbone of any automated processing line.
