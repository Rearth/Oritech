The pulverizer is a slow and simple block that crushes ores and 
biomass into dust. 
It is available early in the tech tree, even before the
[fragment forge](@oritech:fragment_forge_block).

---

**Automated Processing**

Beyond basic ore processing, the pulverizer is commonly used to 
grind biomass for [generators](@oritech:bio_generator_block) or produce 
coal dust for carbon composite materials. 

Small dusts in the output slots will be auto-combined if possible.

It also produces [biomass](@oritech:biomass) which is essential for 
bio-generators.
