The Industrial Chiller is designed to dissipate heat from high-temperature
industrial processes. It is often required for stabilized
[nuclear reactor]($reactor_setup) components or advanced material
cooling.

---

**Environmental Efficiency**

The chiller's performance is highly dependent on its surroundings.
It works twice as fast and with significantly better energy efficiency 
when placed in cold biomes like Snowy Tundra or Frozen Oceans.
