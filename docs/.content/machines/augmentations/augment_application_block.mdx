The Cybernetic Augmentation Center is the primary facility for 
researching and installing permanent upgrades to your body. 
These augmentations provide various benefits, ranging from passive 
stat boosts to entirely new abilities.

<Callout variant="warning">
    Congratulations! If **from the moment you understood the weakness of your flesh, it disgusted you**,
    then you are our ideal customer. You crave the strength and certainty of steel, and *Oritech Inc*
    is happy to oblige.

    Please remember that once you aspire to the purity of the blessed machine, there is no going back.
    All augmentations are permanent, and may cause an inexplicable urge to refer to
    your former peers as "crude biomass."
</Callout>

**Research and Development**

To begin your transformation, simply walk into the assembled **Cybernetic Augmentation Center**. 
This grants access to the research interface, where you can browse and initiate projects. 
Each augmentation requires specific energy and resources, drawn from either your personal inventory or 
the machine's internal storage (accessible via the interface).

Advancement is iterative; many high-tier upgrades require prerequisite research, as visualized in the tech tree.

The center acts as a hub for **Research Stations**, constructed as multiblock add-ons. The main unit supports 
up to **three stations**, allowing for parallel processing.
*   Each station requires **3 Machine Cores**.
*   A station handles one active research task at a time.

**Application and Control**

Once a research is complete, the augmentation becomes available for installation. The procedure requires a one-time resource cost
to permanently fuse the technology with your physiology.

If you already possess cybernetics from another source, you can catalog them into the machine using the **Import** 
function (bottom-left UI button).

**Augmentation Management**
Your enhancements are designed for seamless integration: they require no maintenance or ongoing energy cost. 
However, should an ability prove distracting (like **Ore Vision** or **Flight**), it can be toggled at will.

*   **Toggle Menu**: Hold the augmentation hotkey (**[G]** by default) to open the radial interface. Hover over the desired 
augment and release to switch its state.
*   **Removal**: While augmentations are intended to be permanent, they can be surgically removed if necessary. 
Note that resources invested in the installation are non-refundable.

**Information for Modpack Developers**

Oritech augmentations are fully data-driven and configurable, allowing for extensive customization via datapacks.

*   **Modifier Augments**: Alter entity attributes (e.g., speed, reach, health).
*   **Effect Augments**: Apply status effects similar to potions.
*   **Custom Augments**: Implement unique logic (e.g., Ore Vision). These can be referenced in datapacks but require a Java 
mod for the underlying logic.

Existing augments can be modified or disabled, and completely new ones can be defined via JSON.
*   **Example Definition**: [Attack Damage Augment (GitHub)](https://github.com/Rearth/Oritech/blob/1.21/fabric/src/main/generated/data/oritech/recipe/augment/attackdamage.json)
*   **Texture Location**: `oritech/textures/gui/augment`

Please remember to include translation entries for any custom additions.
