The Cybernetic Augmentation Center is the primary facility for 
researching and installing permanent upgrades to your body. 
These augmentations provide various benefits, ranging from passive 
stat boosts to entirely new abilities.

---

**Research and Development**

Before an augment can be installed, it must be researched. 
This process consumes energy and specific resources based on the 
augmentation's complexity. 
The center utilizes [multiblock research stations]($cyborg_research_stations) 
to process these blueprints.

<Callout variant="info">
    Blueprints are stored within the machine. If the center is 
    destroyed, all unapplied research progress will be lost.
</Callout>

---

**Application and Control**

Once researched, augmentations can be permanently installed for a 
one-time resource cost. 
Many augments can be toggled via the radial interface ([G] by default), 
allowing you to manage active abilities like **Ore Vision** or 
**Flight** on the fly.
