The Quantum Research Station is a high-tier expansion for the 
[Augmentation Center](@oritech:augment_application_block). 
It is required to research endgame augmentations that manipulate 
space-time and high-energy physics.

---

**Advanced Processing**

By using elite [machine cores](@oritech:machine_cores), the quantum 
station enables complex calculations for researches like 
**Flight** and **Hyper Mining Speed**. 
Multiple stations can be attached to a single center to process 
multiple high-tier researches simultaneously.
