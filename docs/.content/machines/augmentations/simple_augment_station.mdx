The Research Station (and its advanced variants) are critical 
components of the [Cybernetic Augmentation Center](@oritech:augment_application_block). 
They provide the necessary computing power to unlock new augmentations.

---

**Tiered Research**

- **Simple Station**: Used for basic physiological improvements.
- **Quantum Research Station**: Required for high-tier upgrades like 
flight and teleportation.
- **Arcane Research Station**: Specializes in mystical augmentations 
that interact with soul energy.

Each station acts as a research slot for the center and requires 
[machine cores](@oritech:machine_cores) to be part of the multiblock 
assembly.
