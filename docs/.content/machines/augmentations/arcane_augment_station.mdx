The Arcane Research Station allows your 
[Augmentation Center](@oritech:augment_application_block) to 
interface with mystical arcane energies.
It is a necessary component for researching "Unholy" augmentations.

It requires **3 machine cores** and fills one augmentation-center station slot, just like the other station types.

This is the tier for the most exotic augments in the tree, including effects such as flight, cloak, portal return, ore vision, gravity control, luck, and other late-game hybrid tech-magic upgrades.
