The Arcane Research Station allows your 
[Augmentation Center](@oritech:augment_application_block) to 
interface with mystical soul energies. 
It is a necessary component for researching "Unholy" augmentations.

---

**Soul Integration**

This station specializes in researches that require 
[soul energy]($souls) and mystical components. 
It must be part of the center's multiblock structure to unlock 
branches of the tech tree related to life-force manipulation and 
arcane protection.
