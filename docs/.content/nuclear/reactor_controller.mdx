The reactor controller is the heart of your reactor. This block needs to be placed once somewhere on the walls.
It needs to be interacted with to assemble or update the reactor.

The GUI shows the current status of the reactor, along with the temperatures of the components and statistics.
The GUI only shows the first layer of the interior.

<Callout variant="danger">
    Always monitor your reactor's heat levels. A sustained overheat will trigger a nuclear meltdown,
    leading to significant structural damage.
</Callout>

For detailed information on reactor mechanics, see the [nuclear reactor guide]($reactor/introduction).
