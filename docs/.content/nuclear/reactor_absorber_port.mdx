title: Reactor Absorber Port
id: oritech:reactor_absorber_port
type: block
---

The reactor absorber port needs to be placed on the top of the reactor, with heat absorbers beneath.

This is where coolant in the form of ice/blue ice/packed ice can be inserted. There's no difference in the cooling power between the different ice variants.

For detailed information on reactor mechanics, see the [nuclear reactor documentation]($NUCLEAR_REACTOR).
