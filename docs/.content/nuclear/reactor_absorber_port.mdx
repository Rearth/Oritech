---
title: Reactor Absorber Port
id: oritech:reactor_absorber_port
type: block
---

The reactor absorber port needs to be placed on the top of the reactor, with heat absorbers beneath.

This is where coolant in the form of ice, blue ice, or packed ice can be inserted. There is no difference in cooling power between the different ice variants.

For detailed information on reactor mechanics, see the [nuclear reactor guide]($reactor/introduction).
