A higher tier variant of the [reactor rod](@oritech:reactor_rod).

Double rods generate 4 internal pulses and consume 2 fuel capacity units per stack height.

They provide significantly higher power density at the cost of exponentially more heat.

For detailed information on reactor mechanics, see the [nuclear reactor guide]($NUCLEAR_REACTOR).
