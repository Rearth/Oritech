The Manhattan Module is a massive explosive device for strategic demolition.

These devices utilize highly enriched nuclear materials to trigger a massive, localized explosion.
They are intended for large-scale landscaping and clearing hostile territory.

They can be triggered with a redstone signal or a nearby explosion. Be warned, there's no delay between the signal
and the explosion.

<Callout variant="danger">
    The resulting crater and blast radius are significant. Ensure you are at a safe distance and have
    properly secured the area before detonation.
</Callout>

There is a config setting called "Boring Nukes" which makes the nuke behave like a block of TNT.
