---
title: Reactor Heat Pipe
id: oritech:reactor_heat_pipe
type: block
---
Heat pipes absorb heat from neighboring fuel rods, and then spread the heat among neighboring components.

The transfer rate is based on the temperature difference between the pipe and its surroundings.

Using heat pipes allows you to route thermal energy from core rod stacks to cooling arrays located further
from the center of the reaction.

For detailed information on reactor mechanics, see the [nuclear reactor guide]($NUCLEAR_REACTOR).
