The Low-Yield Nuclear Explosion Device is a smaller, more controlled version of the Manhattan Module.

While it is less powerful than its larger counterpart, it still packs a significant punch and should be handled with extreme caution. It is perfect for those who require a more localized explosion for landscaping or clearing hostile territory without the massive crater left by a full-scale nuclear device.

It can be triggered with a redstone signal or a nearby explosion. Be warned, there's no delay between the signal and the explosion.

<Callout variant="danger">
    Even with its "low yield," the blast is significantly more powerful than standard TNT. Ensure you have properly
    secured the area and are at a safe distance before detonation.
</Callout>

There's a config setting called "Boring Nukes" which make the device behave like a block of tnt.
