title: Reactor Heat Vent
id: oritech:reactor_vent
type: block
---
Vents remove heat from the hottest neighboring component.

The amount of heat removed is based on the temperature of the neighboring component.

Hotter components result in more heat being removed.

Multiple vents are often required to balance the heat of high-density fuel rod arrays.

For detailed information on reactor mechanics, see the [nuclear reactor guide]($reactor/introduction).
