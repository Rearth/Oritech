The highest tier variant of the [reactor rod](@oritech:reactor_rod).

Quad rods generate 12 internal pulses and consume 4 fuel capacity units per stack height.
They provide the maximum power density at the cost of extreme heat generation.

For detailed information on reactor mechanics, see the [nuclear reactor documentation]($NUCLEAR_REACTOR).
