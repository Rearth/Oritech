The reactor wall is a strong, highly blast-resistant reactor block.
It should encase the entire frame.

These blocks must be arranged in a rectangular prism to properly contain the nuclear reaction.
While most of the shell consists of standard walls, specific positions on the walls and ceiling can be replaced with
[ports](@oritech:reactor_energy_port) to allow for fuel insertion, cooling, and energy extraction.

For detailed information on reactor mechanics, see the [nuclear reactor guide]($reactor/introduction).
