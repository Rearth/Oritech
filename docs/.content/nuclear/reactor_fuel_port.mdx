---
title: Reactor Fuel Port
id: oritech:reactor_fuel_port
type: block
---

Fuel ports need to be placed on the outer ceiling of the reactor, with fuel rods beneath.

This is where fuel pellets can be inserted. The port automatically distributes inserted uranium or plutonium
pellets to the rods below.

When used with item pipes or drones, it enables the creation of indefinitely self-sustaining power plants.
