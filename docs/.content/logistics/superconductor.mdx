The ultimate energy cable for massive RF transfer. Default transfer rate is 4.1M RF/t.

## Usage

Works like standard energy pipes but with much higher throughput limits.
Thicker than regular pipes to reflect the heavy-duty nature. Supercondutors can not connect to regular energy pipes.

## Variants
*   **Standard**: Core superconductor cable
*   **Framed**: Encased for structural integration
*   **Duct**: Alternative aesthetic style. Ducted variants do not connect to machines directly, only to other pipes.

<Callout variant="info">
    Essential for transferring the huge energy output
    of a [Nuclear Reactor](@oritech:reactor_controller).
</Callout>
