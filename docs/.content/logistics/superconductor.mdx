High-end energy cable for very large RF transfer. Default transfer rate is 4.1M RF/t.

## Usage

Works like standard energy pipes but with much higher throughput.
They are thicker than regular pipes. Superconductors cannot connect to regular energy pipes.

## Variants
*   **Standard**: Core superconductor cable
*   **Framed**: Full-block version
*   **Duct**: Alternative aesthetic style. Ducted variants do not connect to machines directly, only to other pipes.

<Callout variant="info">
    Essential for transferring the huge energy output
    of a [Nuclear Reactor](@oritech:reactor_controller).
</Callout>
