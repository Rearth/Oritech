---
title: Lasers And Fluxite
icon: oritech:laser_arm_block
---

Before advancing into many of Oritech's stronger machines, you will need **fluxite**.

The usual way to get it is by using an [Enderic Laser](@oritech:laser_arm_block) with a [Target Designator](@oritech:target_designator) on amethyst clusters.

The laser path is important for more than fluxite. Later on, lasers also power machines such as the [Atomic Forge](@oritech:atomic_forge_block), support wireless energy transfer, and help enable some of the mod's highest-end processing.

With a working laser setup, you are now entering the part of Oritech where machines stop being isolated blocks and start becoming systems.
